How it works

Three steps. No guilt.

We don’t tell you how many you’re allowed. We show you how much stays in your pocket.

1Your usual day

How many cigarettes you have on a normal day and what a pack costs. From that we work out how much you can save.

2Log with a tap

+ Cigarette during the day, or the whole day with one number in the evening. We’ll remind you.

3A payment every morning

We add the difference from your usual day to your vault. Like a paycheck, just for what you didn’t smoke.
