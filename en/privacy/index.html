Privacy policy

Effective from 1 November 2026 · Čeština
In short: Skipjar has no accounts and no server. Everything you enter in the app stays only on your phone. We don’t collect, send or sell anything.

Who runs the app

The Skipjar app is run by [name / company, company ID, address]. Contact: [e-mail].

What data the app processes

  • Cigarette counts by day, cravings that passed, and settings (usual number, price, currency, reminder, appearance).
  • Your vaults: name, target amount, the icon or photo you choose.
  • Badges and your plan, if you use one.

All of it is stored only in the app’s storage on your device. We have no access to it.

What the app doesn’t send

Skipjar contains no analytics, ads, tracking or crash reporting. It uses no accounts and doesn’t talk to any server of ours.

Premium purchase

The purchase and the trial are handled by Google Play. From Google we only learn that Premium is active – no payment details. Google’s processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications – the evening reminder and the Sunday recap. They are created right on your phone; you can turn them off in Settings › Reminder.
  • Choosing a photo – through the system photo picker, without access to your gallery. The app only gets the photo you pick for a vault and stores it, cropped, in the app’s storage.
  • Vibration – gentle feedback when you tap. No prompt, nothing is stored.
  • Run at startup – so the reminder is scheduled again after your phone restarts.
  • Internet – only to buy and verify Premium through Google Play. None of your data goes anywhere through it.

Backup and sharing

Your phone can back up the app’s data through Google (Android Backup) if you have it turned on – Google manages that backup under your account and we have no access to it. You can also save a backup to a file of your choice. Vault photos are not included in backups, and a Premium purchase is restored through Google Play. Sharing a picture of a vault or a week goes through the system share sheet – you send it to whoever you want.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data in Settings › Privacy and about Skipjar › Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app. A Premium purchase stays with your Google Play account.

Age

The app is intended for people over 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll publish the new version on this page with a new date.
